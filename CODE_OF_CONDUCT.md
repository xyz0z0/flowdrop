# Code of Conduct

Treat other participants with respect in FlowDrop issues, discussions, pull
requests and reviews. Give feedback on the work and assume others may have
different experience, perspectives or ways of communicating.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention and
personal attacks are grounds for moderation. Get permission before sharing
someone else's private information or correspondence.

Use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
to report abuse. GitHub forwards reports to the appropriate team. Share only the
information needed to identify what happened. For software vulnerabilities, see
the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation based on the severity
and frequency of the conduct. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion.
